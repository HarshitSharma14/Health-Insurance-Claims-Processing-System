Low back pain, radiating to right leg

Technique

MRI of the lumbar spine was performed on a 3.0 Tesla Siemens MAGNETOM Vida scanner. Sequences: Sagittal T1W, T2W, STIR; Axial T2W at L3-S1 levels. No contrast administered.

Findings

Vertebral bodies: Normal height and signal intensity. No compression fracture or marrow edema.

L3-L4: Mild disc desiccation. Minimal posterior disc bulge. No significant canal or foraminal stenosis.

L4-L5: Moderate disc desiccation with posterior disc bulge and bilateral facet hypertrophy causing mild bilateral neural foraminal narrowing. No significant central canal stenosis.

L5-S1: Broad-based posterior disc herniation (approximately 5 mm protrusion) causing moderate left lateral recess stenosis with compression of the traversing left S1 nerve root. Mild right foraminal narrowing.

Posterior elements: No spondylolysis or spondylolisthesis. Facet joints show bilateral hypertrophic changes at L4-L5 and L5-S1.

Paraspinal soft tissues: Unremarkable.

Conus medullaris: Terminates normally at L1 level.

Impression / Conclusion

  1. L5-S1 broad-based posterior disc herniation with moderate left lateral recess stenosis and compression of left S1 nerve root — consistent with Lumbar Disc Herniation.
  2. L4-L5 posterior disc bulge with mild bilateral foraminal narrowing.
  3. Multilevel lumbar spondylosis.

Clinical correlation recommended. Neurosurgery / Spine surgery referral advised if symptoms persist.
